Notion → Website Sync

Pulls whatever's new or updated in Notion (Learn Articles, About, Work With Me) and publishes it to the site. Runs automatically every day — no action needed.

To publish something right now instead of waiting for the daily run:

  1. Open the sync workflow on GitHub (button below).
  2. Click the "Run workflow" dropdown, then the green "Run workflow" button.
  3. It finishes in under a minute — refresh the page to see it complete.
