* The catch-all book is an incredible tool for learning and time management.
* There are two main purposes for my use:

+ Daily tasks and ideas
+ Notes from things I learn about

* I will reach for this journal first to capture all the thoughts, which gives me the benefits of handwriting. The real benefit, however, comes from when I review the book to synthesize, reword, and clarify my thoughts at the end of the day or week.
* Many people try the notebook thing because they find it aesthetic or satisfying, but without a habit of reviewing every day or weekly, you're severely missing out and not going to engrain it in your brain. This is why I have Notion, because it gives me an excuse to review and actually rewrite my notes, and continuously review them as well.
