* Sometimes you have a problem that just keeps coming up in your work or life. You've tried different solutions but none of them seem to work for long.
* You've been treating the symptom, not the disease.
* Some examples

+ Campaigns feel inconsistent - Symptom (assumed problem): Create templates and stricter boundaries for the team
+ Root cause (actual problem): The team doesn't share a vision for what the work is supposed to be
+ Actual solution: Have alignment sessions and build consistency through values and principles, not templates
+ The client keeps making changes - Proposed solution: Keep making changes or limit to a certain number of changes
+ Root cause: The client generally knows what they want, but you weren't aligned strategically from the start
+ Actual solution: Spend more time upfront discussing the strategic vision, not the project details
+ Deadlines are continuously missed - Proposed solution: Better time management, stricter schedules and more progress-tracking metrics
+ Root cause: Priorities constantly change and people can't work long enough on one to make progress
+ Actual solution: Spend more time clarifying and defining priorities and focus on finishing, not shifting projects

* To figure it out:
* Find the root cause

+ State the problem you're noticing, then ask "Why?" 5 times
+ The last answer will most likely be the root cause for the presenting symptom
+ You can keep asking if you feel there's still more under the surface, but usually not necessary

* Brainstorm the solution

+ With your final answer, look at every aspect that plays a role, and try to find a solution
+ For example: Symptom: I procrastinate and miss deadlines
+ Root cause: I dislike the tasks and don't know how to do them
+ Brainstormed solutions: Find someone that likes doing those tasks; Hire someone for it; Learn how to do them better; Make the tasks more enjoyable; Use task-management techniques to prioritize; Set rules to finish those tasks before anything else

* Choose the solution

+ If your problem needs to urgently be solved, or you just want a short-term solution, pick the one that requires the least amount of effort/investment, and test it out
+ If you want lasting or more meaningful change, try and start with yourself, which option would make you a better person? Or look to the company or agency as a whole, which option would bring you closer to your long term vision?
