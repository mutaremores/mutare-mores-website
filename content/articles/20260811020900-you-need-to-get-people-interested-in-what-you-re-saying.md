  - Don’t just start explaining things. Assume that they don’t care and that they’re not going to continue listening. You have to convince them.
---
---

- When you want someone to listen to what you have to say, convince them of something, sell your idea, etc., you need to focus on how to get their attention.
- Why should they listen to you? How is it relevant to them? 
- You might have a good or valuable idea, but that’s only half the battle. You need to explain yourself better, but in order to do that you first need to have their attention *throughout* the conversation, not just the hook.
