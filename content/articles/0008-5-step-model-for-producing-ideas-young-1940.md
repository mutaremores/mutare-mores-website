tldr: |
  * This is the classic model for defining what the creative process is.
  * Created by James Webb Young, an advertising executive in 1940
  * 1. Gather - collect the necessary materials and data on the problem
  * 2. Digest - look at it from all angles and fully understand it
  * 3. Process - take a break from thinking about it
  * 4. Eureka - the moment the idea "appears out of nowhere"
  * 5. Reality - look at the idea critically and make it real if it's good
