**What it means**

- Reads emotional cues — tone, body language, what's not being said — in real time, including in tense or high-stakes moments
- Regulates their own reactions under pressure rather than being visibly rattled by them
- Adjusts approach based on how a message is likely to land for a specific person, not just its content
- Notices when someone on the team is struggling before it's announced

**Strengths**

- Builds trust quickly — people feel heard and understood
- Reads the room and adjusts approach in most situations, including tense ones
- Effective at defusing conflict before it escalates
- Gets more honest, complete information from people because they feel safe bringing it

**Focus on**

- Noticing when emotional investment is slowing down a decision that needs to be made quickly and cleanly
- Practicing directness — delivering a hard message clearly, without over-cushioning it to protect the other person's feelings
- Setting boundaries around absorbing other people's stress, so it doesn't become a personal cost
- Watching for a bias toward harmony over necessary conflict, especially in decisions with a clear right answer
