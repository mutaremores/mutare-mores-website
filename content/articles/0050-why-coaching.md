* The why:

+ Most people experience coaching through sports when they were younger, and while that's definitely one form of coaching, it's completely different when the focus is purely on you and your goals.
+ Coaching is such a unique experience on both sides. One gets clarity and the other gets satisfaction.
+ It feels like I've been on several career paths my whole adult life. I always liked the fact that I didn't really need to settle for one, but that's exactly what made it so difficult. With coaching, I'm able to take all my experience and interests and bring a unique perspective to the conversations I have with clients.
+ With coaching, I'm able to bring it just about anywhere using calls and video meetings. I am someone that gets fulfillment from traveling abroad and experiencing the culture, so for me, coaching acts as a way for me to engrain myself with the people (and even practice the language!).

* The story:

+ Once I got my master's degree in organizational psychology, I was in a training and development position, in the HR department of a local credit union. This allowed me to learn and grow exponentially, on my own time.
+ I knew I loved helping others, so I always focus on that in a career, and I knew I wasn't going to be able to afford staying in this position for long.
+ Consulting is a huge pathway for most IO Psychology grads, and I definitely tried, oftentimes multiple applications to the same company or firm, without a single interview.
+ I noticed coaching a few times in some textbooks or resources, and eventually started researching what exactly it is. I thought it was limited to career and life coaching, little did I know there's a coach for just about everything.
+ Over the course of several weeks, I slowly started implementing coaching into my actual work, doing it for the leaders in the credit union and just general conversations with people I met.
+ I LOVED it. The conversations would flow, the people would get insights and clarity that they didn't realize before, and all I did was ask the right questions.
+ Since this brought about changes that saved or earned more money, I knew I would eventually start charging for it. I now needed to create a business.
