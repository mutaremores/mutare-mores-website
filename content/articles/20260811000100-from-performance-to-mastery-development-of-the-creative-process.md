- In this article by Glenn Griffin (2008), he studied the creative process of beginner and advanced advertising students to better understand how they came up with creative ads.
- They were given creative briefs to then create ads for in the same day

### 4 dimensions of the creative process

1. Orientation for the work
  1. Tip: Don’t categorize yourself and your thinking to what you are (e.g. “I’m an advertiser, I am making an ad”)
1. Approach to the problem
  1. Tip: Discover the problem that YOU FIND, not what they are telling you
1. Mindscribing
  1. Tip: Be free and write as much as you can, don’t filter out thoughts or ideas, this is the fuel for your creative ideas
1. Heuristics
  1. Gain more experience to build your own “rules of thumb”
