* Being a creative Leader means you are working with people who are usually freelancers, or, being creative themselves, not the stereotypical employee.
* The result of your leadership, the film, the photoshoot, the production, it all leads back to how you lead the people working together for the same goal
* There are those who like rules and structure, and then there are those who do not. Creatives, obviously, fall into the latter. This means that the more you try to manage, the less likely you are going to have the result you want from them.
* When a creative is told what to do exactly, how to do it, micromanaged, or given feedback in a way that doesn't work for them, it takes a little piece of their heart out of the project.
* When you get a bunch of freelancers and out-of-the-box thinkers, you need to also be an out-of-the-box leader.
* Creatives come off as difficult to manage, simply because they should not, in fact, be managed. They need to be guided towards your vision in a way that emphasizes their strengths and supports them in their weaknesses. This requires vision-based communication, guidance, and a servant leadership mindset.
* If you feel like you are doing everything right but the team just isn't creating in the way you imagined, try and think about what you could change about your leadership first.
