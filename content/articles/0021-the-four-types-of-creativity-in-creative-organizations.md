* In an organization based on creativity, there is a difference in the creative functions of the leader, the team members, and the organization as a whole
* Personal creativity

+ This is what first comes to mind when we think of the word creativity.
+ This can come in the form of skill (like a painter or singer), or in thinking (like a good leader or strategist).
+ Your aim should be to improve your creative skill and thinking, for both yourself and your team members. Always be learning, stay curious and open, and don't let the fear of mistakes hold you back from learning.

* Leader creativity

+ Leaders of creative teams need to lead everyone towards a shared vision, and this requires creativity, but not in the sense of artistic creativity, like a photographer or designer would have, because a leader is not there to create, they are there to help others create.
+ Leadership creativity comes in the form of finding unique ways to inspire and lead. A more strategic viewpoint, where you are able to coordinate efforts of everyone towards a goal, capitalizing on your team's strengths (and even weaknesses).

* Team creativity

+ Teams need to be creative in how they work together, how they communicate, and brainstorm.
+ There is the artistic creativity of the individual team member (the videographer, designer, etc.), but their full potential can only be realized if the team works well together.
+ Without good teamwork, teams can only produce at the average level of creativity of the team, but good teams should be wanting to maximize their potential, which is where their collective creativity and collaboration come into play.

* Organization creativity

+ Creative organizations need to be creative in their systems and approaches to the work they do.
+ Internally this creativity can come in the form of structure and organization of the teams themselves, the rules, the office space, the communication channels used, and more.
+ Externally, this creativity comes in the form of how clients are handled, the marketing strategy.
