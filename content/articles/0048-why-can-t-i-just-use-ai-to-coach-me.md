* Let's start with what coaching is not:

+ Coaching is not to give you advice
+ Coaching is not "here's my problem, fix it"
+ Coaching is not therapy
+ Coaching is not a clear, set-in-stone process or method

* If any of these surprised you, more detail:

+ Giving advice is more consulting, which, while I do work in some consulting or advice-giving into my work, that isn't all the time, and shouldn't be the focus of the conversation
+ I do not fix people's problems, or achieve their goals, only they can do that. I do, however, help them get to that "aha!" moment, where things become clear, and they finally get to peak through the fog that has been clouding their mind
+ I do not work with trauma or mental health issues, not only am I not qualified, but the point of coaching is to look into the future, not the past.
+ Coaching has a wide variety of methodologies and frameworks to help people, but good coaching does not rely on any of these anyways, a good coach will be able to help the client whether it's a conversation at a bar, hiking up a mountain, or sitting in a coffee shop on a video call

* Now, why can't AI just do these things for you? AI is a network of words and information, and with the way it's structured, it identifies patterns in this information. When you start a sentence, there are words that are more likely to come next, and words that are not. This is how it creates all its responses, based on patterns from what was said before. No new information, just a mix and match of the old that seems new and personal.
* You can go on ChatGPT right now and ask it to coach you, and feel good about the questions and responses. The problem isn't whether AI can coach you, because it definitely can, the problem is that AI does not coach well.
* Good coaching usually provides a couple things that AI simply cannot:

+ Accountability
+ Experience

* AI is only as useful as the person using it. Good coaching asks you questions you might not want to answer, and gives you responses you might not want to hear, but need to. When you get coaching, you're getting someone that will not only support you, but hold you accountable to achieving your goals.
* Coaches also bring experience. AI has vast knowledge about every topic, but that usually results in impersonal, overwhelming, and just plain irrelevant responses. Coaches have real-world experience with the problems you are facing.
* A more subtle impact of coaching is on the subconscious level. You are building a bond with another human that cannot be replicated by AI. Only a real human can make you feel truly accepted and heard in a way that brings about lasting change.
