  - This is a framework for evaluation how effective a training program is, usually used in the context of job and skill training given to employees. 
  1. Reactions of participants, do they enjoy or find it useful?
  1. Do they actually learn it?
  1. Do they actually apply what they learn?
  1. Is there an impact for the organization’s goals?
    1. Revenue, satisfaction, etc.
  1. (An addition was made later for ROI, or evaluating whether the impact was worth the cost/ investment of the training)
