tldr: |
  * This is a framework for evaluation how effective a training program is, usually used in the context of job and skill training given to employees.
  * 1. Reactions of participants, do they enjoy or find it useful?
  * 2. Do they actually learn it?
  * 3. Do they actually apply what they learn?
  * 4. Is there an impact for the organization's goals?
  
  + Revenue, satisfaction, etc.
  
  * 5. (An addition was made later for ROI, or evaluating whether the impact was worth the cost/ investment of the training)
