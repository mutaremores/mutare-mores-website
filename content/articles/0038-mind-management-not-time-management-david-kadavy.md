* Many places have a completely different relationship with time

+ They are event-based, not time-based
+ Instead of thinking about 11:30am, they will say "after lunch" or "after the previous meeting"
+ While they might not be as productive and aren't constantly busy, they tend to be happier and more satisfied with life

* Your prefrontal cortex is the "CEO" of your brain, it likes rules and structure

+ When it is less active, you are in a more creative state
+ This tends to be in the morning, when you're groggy, but this is different than at night when you are tired
+ You still need sleep to process information, so don't stay up late all the time
+ Avoid coffee and stimulants, embrace the grogginess to work on creative tasks
+ The author implemented a rule to do the creative work for the first hour in the morning

* Important tasks first, think of filling a jar with rocks, then gravel, then sand

+ You can't fit the important tasks (the rocks) if you fill your day with small ones (the sand)

* Stages of creativity

+ 1. Preliminary work - Research and get to know the problem at hand from all angles
+ 2. Rest - You can't go straight into creative thinking, give your brain time to rest and then think
+ 3. Creativity - This is why "Shower Thoughts" are a thing, your brain is forced to "zone out" and that's when it processes things
+ Distracting yourself (social media is a big one, of course) prevents this processing
