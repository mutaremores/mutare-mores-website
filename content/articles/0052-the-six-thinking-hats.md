* From Dr. Edward de Bono's book "Six Thinking Hats", originally published in 1985
* When you are stuck on a decision or problem, try this strategy to think about it from various perspectives
* Look at it through the perspective of each hat, taking notes on what comes to you for each one
* Throughout the process of doing this, the issue will become clearer in your mind and you should have a much better mental space to make a sound decision
* Example: I am struggling to decide on whether to go forward with a new social media campaign

+ Blue hat - Review the market data, then brainstorm ideas freely, evaluate the strongest concepts, identify risks, and finish by assigning responsibilities and creating a project timeline
+ Green hat - Instead of relying on user-generated content, what if we partnered with local businesses? We could also create a partnership with them and give them discounts on our services
+ White hat - Our last three social media campaigns increased engagement by 18% on average. We have a budget of $10,000 and four weeks before launch.
+ Yellow hat - If this campaign succeeds, we'll increase brand awareness, strengthen customer engagement, and give team members an opportunity to showcase their creativity.
+ Red hat - I have a good feeling about this concept. It feels authentic to our brand, but I'm concerned the team is already feeling stretched and could become overwhelmed with this deadline
+ Black hat - If we launch without enough testing, we could receive negative feedback. The timeline is tight, and missing deadlines could affect the quality of the campaign
