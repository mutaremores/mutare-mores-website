* Leonardo Da Vinci, Michael Jordan, Einstein, Napoleon, Alexander the Great, Michael Jackson, Shakespeare, Beethoven. These are examples of some of the greatest humans to ever exist. They didn't just break a record or discover something new, they made a worldwide impact.
* I've thought about what it takes to achieve greatness quite a bit, it's so uncommon, but what is it that separates them from all the other "great", notable, and incredible people who make an impact in the world?
* Of course these are my own thoughts, no research or books were involved in this thought, but I feel that there are several components of greatness.
* Greatness - an impact on the world so great that you are remembered for generations to come, written in the history books

+ Suffering - The immediate result of something extremely difficult, challenging, or stressful
+ Goals - Something you desire that you do not currently have, both physical and conceptual
+ Sacrifice - Willingly suffering for the sake of one's goals
+ Ambition - The desire that determines how much you are willing to sacrifice
+ Resilience - When ambition to achieve goals > the sacrifice required

* There is no such thing as a life without suffering. The people who achieve greatness, however, suffered because of their sacrifice. So it seems like: ambition + goals + sacrifice = greatness
* The caveat is that your goals cannot be normal goals. Great people want to change the world, not just build a big company or do something good for the world. So let's add "scope of goals": ambition + scope of goals + sacrifice = greatness
* But then you need to consider the environmental factors, none of these people existed in a vacuum: ambition + scope of goals + sacrifice + environment = greatness
* I'm sure there are plenty of other caveats and exceptions, but for the most part, I feel that this encompasses what it means to be great. You need high ambition, large-scale goals, be willing to sacrifice more than is comfortable, and if the environment and world around you is ready for it, you might just become great.
