* Training should be:

+ Continuous - Always be learning, if your training stops after onboarding, you are severely limiting your organization's potential (and your employee's personal growth!)
+ Accessible - It shouldn't be a hassle to conduct or review
+ Performance focused - Don't just teach, get them to actually perform
+ If it's a simple task, at least 50 repetitions is needed to be proficient
+ Supported by leaders - If leaders don't see the value, why would anyone support it or try to follow along? Leaders likely won't do the training properly anyways.
+ A safe space - For questions, trying new things, and learning without judgment.

* Amount of training by type

+ On-the-job training - 70%
+ Social learning/ other training - 20%
+ Formal videos/ programs - 10%
