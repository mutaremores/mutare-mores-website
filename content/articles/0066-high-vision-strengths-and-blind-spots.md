**What it means**

- Holds a clear picture of where things are headed and returns to it often, not just at kickoff meetings or annual planning
- Naturally connects today's decisions back to a longer-term direction
- Communicates the "why" behind the work in a way that gives people something to orient around during uncertainty
- Thinks in terms of where the team/business needs to be in a year or more, not just this quarter

**Strengths**

- Anchors the team during ambiguity with a clear sense of where things are headed
- Vision consistently shapes day-to-day decisions, not just big-picture ones
- Exceptional at aligning people around a future state they can't yet see
- Gives people a reason to push through short-term friction because the destination is clear

**Focus on**

- Checking in on present-day problems that are easy to wave past in service of the bigger story — a real complaint today deserves a real answer today
- Translating the vision into concrete near-term steps for people who need something tangible, not just direction
- Staying close enough to daily operations that the vision doesn't start to feel disconnected from what the team is actually experiencing
- Confirming that managers and team members are actually aligned with the stated direction, rather than assuming buy-in because the vision has been communicated
