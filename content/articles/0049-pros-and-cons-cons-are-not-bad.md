* Cons means considerations, it doesn't necessarily mean they are the "negative side effects" or the "dark side" of a choice, just the things that you need to consider.
* Thinking about it this way will help frame cons in your mind in a way that isn't just the "I think that would suck", but instead "That would be something I'd need to consider if I chose this".
