**What it means**

- Turns plans into finished outcomes reliably, including when volume or pressure picks up
- Has systems and habits (not just willpower) that keep work moving even when things get busy
- Treats a plan as something to be delivered, not just discussed
- Tracks follow-through closely — commitments made are commitments tracked to completion

**Strengths**

- Exceptionally dependable — consistent output even under high volume or pressure
- Systems and habits hold up when things get busy, not just when conditions are calm
- Reliably turns vague goals into concrete next steps
- Gives a team confidence that what gets committed to actually gets delivered

**Focus on**

- Building in a deliberate checkpoint to ask "is this still the right plan," rather than pushing through momentum alone
- Making space for creative input mid-execution instead of treating the plan as fixed once it's in motion
- Watching for process becoming the priority over the people or outcomes it's meant to serve
- Noticing when a plan needs to break — persistence is a strength until it's protecting a decision that's already wrong
