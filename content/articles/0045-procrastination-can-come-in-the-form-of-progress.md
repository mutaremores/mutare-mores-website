  * Building up my business, I made progress nearly every day. I had new ideas, made new resources or strategies, but... they never got me closer to my goal.
  * It was busywork, just distractions disguised as being productive.
  * I'm not saying these things didn't matter or didn't help at all, but I was focusing on the 20% tasks, not the ones that would give me 80% of the results
  * I made new websites and planned out videos, but the only thing I actually needed to do was talk to people and post videos. Everything else was an improvement, but not progress.
