* Decision-making is something we do every day (even though some people claim up to 35,000, I don't know if I agree). Some are obviously more important and require more cognitive effort than others, but most of them are rather simple like choosing which pen to write with or what to say to someone
* The problem is, when decisions become hard, they can become really hard. Think about moral dilemmas, white lies, or potentially life-altering decisions. This is where learning about how to make a better decision comes into play. We are naturally good at making quick decisions thanks to Heuristics, but anything beyond that requires thought, and the better you are at thinking about the issue at hand, the happier you will be with the decisions you make.
* There's different aspects to decision making, so it's important to know what part you have trouble with.
