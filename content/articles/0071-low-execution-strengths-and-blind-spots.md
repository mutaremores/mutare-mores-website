**What it means**

- Plans and good intentions don't reliably turn into finished outcomes, especially once real pressure or volume shows up
- Follow-through tends to hold up when conditions are calm and falls off when things get busy
- Comfortable abandoning an approach that isn't working, sometimes before giving it a full chance to
- Tends to rely on motivation in the moment rather than systems that carry the work when motivation dips

**Strengths**

- Comfortable pivoting away from a plan that isn't working
- Doesn't get overly attached to a single approach or process
- Open to changing direction without much friction
- Quick to recognize when something genuinely isn't working, rather than pushing through out of stubbornness

**Focus on**

- Building a simple system (even a basic one — a checklist, a weekly review) rather than relying on remembering everything
- Committing to fewer things at once, so what does get committed to has a real chance of being finished
- Noticing the pattern of abandoning a plan under pressure specifically, and building in extra structure for exactly those moments
- Separating "this isn't working" from "this is hard" before deciding to change direction
