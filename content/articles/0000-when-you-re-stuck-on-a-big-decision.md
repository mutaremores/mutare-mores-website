The following exercise seems obvious until you actually do it. It forces yourself to realize the subconscious thought patterns about your career and what you actually want in life.

1. On a sheet of paper, write 4 columns: Physical, creative, social, and mental.

2. Visualize where you want to be in life in 5-10 years. Not just your job, but your lifestyle, family, hobbies, etc.

3. Now translate that into "I am..." sentences for each category. Make a list of 10 for each.

(e.g. Physical - I am playing with my kids or I am still running marathons)

4. Now, draw a line below the list and take a step back and WRITE DOWN the answers to the following:

- What patterns do you see? What salary, schedule, lifestyle, and other requirements are there for this life to be true?

5. Now, draw another line and make a note in each column about how the career move that your considering would affect that category: Will it bring you closer to that goal? Or hold you back?
